Advanced Manufacturing Capabilities

Riders combines cutting-edge technology with a highly skilled workforce to deliver superior 100% cotton yarn and fabric. Our fully integrated operations — from cotton ginning to spinning, weaving, and recycling — ensure consistent quality, sustainability, and efficiency at every stage of production.

GINNING SERVICES

Our ginning facilities are equipped with cutting-edge SUMER BRAND machines, ensuring the efficient separation of cotton fibers from seeds. This critical process guarantees high-quality input materials for further processing. In addition to meeting our internal needs, we are renowned for providing exceptional ginning services to external
customers.
