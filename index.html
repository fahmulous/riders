Riders Trading and Manufacturing S.C.

For over three years, Riders Trading and Manufacturing S.C. has been a catalyst for Ethiopia's industrial growth. As one of the nation's fastest-growing investment share companies, we operate across five key sectors: Textile Manufacturing, Agriculture, Real Estate & Construction, Financial Services, and Healthcare. Our businesses are deeply woven into the fabric of Ethiopia's economy—creating jobs, advancing industries, and elevating the nation's global competitiveness. Through strategic investments, world-class quality, and a deep commitment to the communities we serve, Riders continues to build a stronger, more prosperous Ethiopia.

Textile Industry

Vertically integrated textile manufacturing in Adama: cotton ginning (20 tons/day), spinning, weaving (35,000+ meters/day), and recycling operations serving local and international markets.

Farming

Large-scale farming operations: Amibara (400ha operational), Gewane (3,000ha, $34M investment), and South Omo (2,000ha). Growing cotton, cereals, oilseeds, and export crops.

Real Estate & Construction

Strategic real estate development and construction projects contributing to Ethiopia's infrastructure growth and urban development initiatives.

Financial Sectors

Strategic partnerships and investments in Ethiopia's financial sector including Zamzam Bank, Hijra Bank, and Amal Microfinance, expanding access to Islamic and conventional banking services.
