Our Mission, Vision & Values

Our Capabilities Across the Textile Value Chain

Ginning

Daily 20 tons of high-quality cotton lint, produced with advanced machinery for efficiency and consistency.

Spinning

Ring and open-end yarn production, over 20 tons daily, in multiple counts and quality grades.

Weaving

Versatile fabric output of more than 35,000 meters per day — woven and knitted grey fabrics in diverse sizes.

Recycling

A modern cotton recycling unit turning textile waste into new raw material, advancing sustainability in Ethiopia.

From Ethiopia to the World: Our Textile Journey

Founded in 2021, Riders S.C. began with a vision to transform Ethiopia’s industrial landscape. The strategic acquisition of ELSE Textile in 2022 enabled us to create a fully integrated textile operation — from raw cotton to finished fabrics. Today, we combine modern machinery, sustainable practices, and skilled professionals to serve both local and international markets.
