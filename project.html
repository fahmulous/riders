Our Strategic Projects

Textiles

Riders Trading & Manufacturing S.C. entered the textile sector with the strategic acquisition of ELSE Textile Industry in 2022. This facility is a fully integrated textile factory located in Adama, Ethiopia.

Current Operations

  • Ginning: The facility has a modern ginning mill with a daily production capacity of 20 tons of lint cotton.
  • Spinning: It is equipped with both ring frame and open-end spinning systems, producing over 20 tons of yarn daily.
  • Weaving: The factory offers woven and knitted gray fabric, with a daily output of more than 35,000 meters. The facility also features in-house recycling units for sustainability.

Expansion Projects

Riders plans to expand our textile operations to create a fully integrated apparel value chain. This includes:

  • Wet Processing: A new line will be established for bleaching, dyeing, and printing.
  • Garment Manufacturing: A facility is being planned for end-to-end apparel production, including design, cutting, stitching, and finishing.

Amal Microfinance

Riders Trading & Manufacturing S.C. is involved in the financial sector through its investment project with Amal Microfinance Institution Share Company (AMFISC). AMFISC is the first licensed Interest-Free Microfinance Institution in Ethiopia, legally recognized by the National Bank of Ethiopia (License No. MFI/056/2023) and registered with Addis Ababa City Administration Trade Bureau.

Established by 574 shareholders, AMFISC began with an authorized capital of 68,069,000 birr and a paid-up capital of 34,698,265 birr. The institution is fully Shariah-compliant and is dedicated to advancing financial inclusion by providing innovative microfinance solutions to underserved communities across Ethiopia.
