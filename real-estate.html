BUILDING FOR GROWTH

Strategic Real Estate Development

Riders Real Estate & Construction represents our investment in Ethiopia's rapidly growing property and infrastructure sectors. As part of our diversified portfolio, we develop commercial, residential, and industrial properties that meet the needs of Ethiopia's expanding economy.

With a focus on quality construction, sustainable design, and strategic location selection, we're creating value for shareholders while contributing to Ethiopia's urban development. Our projects range from commercial office spaces and residential developments to industrial facilities supporting our textile and agricultural operations.
