Building Ethiopia's Financial Future

As part of our diversified investment strategy, Riders Trading and Manufacturing S.C. maintains strategic equity stakes in three leading Ethiopian financial institutions. These investments, totaling ETB 20.5 million, position us within Ethiopia's rapidly expanding financial services sector while generating stable returns for our shareholders.

Our portfolio includes shareholdings in two commercial banks and one microfinance institution, all operational and serving millions of Ethiopians. These strategic investments contribute to financial inclusion, economic development, and provide stable dividend income to support our overall business objectives.

Key Investment Stats:

  • 3 Institutions - Banks & Microfinance
  • 100% Operational - All Investments Active
  • Nationwide - Serving Ethiopian Markets

Strategic Stakes in Financial Institutions

Amal Microfinance

Our Investment:

  • Investment Year: 2023–2024
  • Status: Operational

Amal Microfinance Institution specializes in providing financial services to underserved communities, small businesses, and micro-enterprises across Ethiopia. The institution plays a vital role in financial inclusion by offering microloans, savings products, and financial literacy programs to low-income individuals and entrepreneurs.

Services:

  • Microloans for small businesses
  • Group lending and solidarity financing
  • Savings mobilization services
  • Agricultural financing
  • Women's empowerment programs
  • Financial literacy and training

Strategic Value:

  • Supporting financial inclusion and social impact
  • Access to grassroots financial networks
  • Diversified investment in microfinance sector
  • Contributing to economic development in rural areas
  • Stable returns from growing microfinance industry
Zamzam Bank

Strategic Value:

  • Investment Year: 2023–2024
  • Status: Operational

Zamzam Bank is one of Ethiopia's emerging commercial banks, providing comprehensive banking services including corporate banking, retail banking, and trade finance. The bank operates a growing branch network serving individual customers, businesses, and corporate clients across Ethiopia.

Services:

  • Corporate and commercial banking
  • Retail banking and savings accounts
  • Trade finance and foreign exchange
  • Digital banking services
  • SME financing

Strategic Value:

  • Access to banking services for Riders' operations
  • Dividend income from profitable banking operations
  • Exposure to Ethiopia's growing financial sector
  • Strategic banking relationships supporting business growth
  • Long-term capital appreciation potential
Hijra Bank

Strategic Value:

  • Investment Year: 2023–2024
  • Status: Operational

Hijra Bank operates as an interest-free Islamic banking institution in Ethiopia, serving customers who prefer Sharia-compliant financial services. The bank provides ethical banking solutions based on Islamic finance principles, including profit-sharing, leasing, and partnership-based financing models.

Services:

  • Interest-free Islamic banking
  • Murabaha (cost-plus financing)
  • Mudarabah (profit-sharing)
  • Ijarah (leasing arrangements)
  • Sharia-compliant savings and investment accounts

Strategic Value:

  • Diversification into Islamic finance sector
  • Access to Sharia-compliant financing options
  • Growing market for ethical banking in Ethiopia
  • Stable returns through profit-sharing models
  • Strategic financial partnerships for Riders' operations

Strategic Benefits for Riders Shareholders

Investment Rationale

Why We Invest in Financial Services:

  • Portfolio Diversification - Balance operational businesses (textile, agriculture, real estate) with financial sector investments, reducing concentration risk
  • Stable Dividend Income - Regular distributions from profitable banking and micro-finance operations provide consistent cash flow
  • High-Growth Sector - Ethiopia's financial services sector is expanding rapidly with banking penetration increasing and new regulations supporting growth
  • Strategic Access - Direct banking relationships facilitate working capital loans, trade finance, and foreign exchange services for Riders' operations
  • Long-Term Capital Appreciation - Equity stakes in growing financial institutions offer significant value appreciation potential as the sector matures
  • Economic Participation - Contributing to Ethiopia's financial development and inclusion while generating returns for shareholders

Shareholder Benefits

Value for Riders Shareholders:

  • Recurring Revenue Stream - Annual dividends from bank profits supplement income from textile manufacturing and farming operations
  • Capital Growth - Increasing value of financial sector stakes as Ethiopian banking sector expands
  • Risk Mitigation - Financial investments provide portfolio balance against operational risks in manufacturing and agriculture
  • Liquidity Potential - Listed financial institutions offer future exit opportunities and tradeable equity positions
  • Synergy Benefits - Banking services support Riders' core businesses with preferential access to credit facilities and financial products
  • Sector Exposure - Shareholders gain indirect exposure to Ethiopia's high-growth financial services industry
