Riders Farming & Agriculture represents our strategic investment in Ethiopia's agricultural transformation. Operating across three major regions—Afar, Somali, and Southern Nations—we are developing large-scale commercial farming operations with total land capacity exceeding 10,000 hectares.

Our integrated approach combines cotton production (supplying our textile operations), food security crops (wheat, cereals, oilseeds), and high-value export crops (coffee, fruits, vegetables). With modern irrigation infrastructure and sustainable farming practices, we're contributing to Ethiopia's agricultural modernization while creating employment and economic opportunities in rural communities.

Project Objectives

Textile Industry Integration

Our primary objective is to supply high-quality cotton to Riders-ELSE Textile Industry—a functioning and viable factory located in Adama, owned by Riders Trading and Manufacturing S.C.—and for the international export market.

  • Direct farm-to-factory supply chain
  • Quality-controlled cotton production
  • Reduced raw material costs for textile operations
  • Export surplus to international markets
  • Vertical integration competitive advantage

Export-Quality Agricultural Products

To produce various types of agricultural products such as fruits, oilseeds, coffee, cereals, and other exportable crops for domestic consumption and international markets.

  • High-value export crops (coffee, oilseeds, fruits)
  • Food security crops (wheat, cereals)
  • Specialty products (hybrid seeds, vegetables)
  • Foreign currency earnings
  • Market diversification and risk management
