Textile Overview

Riders-ELSE Textile Industry, located in Adama Industrial Park, represents our flagship manufacturing operation and the cornerstone of Riders Trading and Manufacturing S.C.'s diversified portfolio. Established as a vertically integrated textile facility, we control every stage of production—from raw cotton processing to finished fabric—ensuring quality, efficiency, and competitive pricing."

With production capacity of 35,000+ meters per day and cotton ginning capacity of 20 tons daily, we serve both Ethiopian domestic markets and international export customers. Our facility employs 600+ skilled workers and operates state-of-the-art machinery across four key production lines.

Key Stats:

  • 35,000+ meters/day - Weaving Capacity
  • 20 tons/day - Ginning Capacity
  • 600+ employees - Skilled Workforce
  • Export Ready - International Quality

Products and Services

Cotton Ginning

  • 20 tons per day
  • Raw cotton processing and fiber separation from seeds. Our ginning operation supplies both our own spinning line and external customers, ensuring consistent quality cotton fiber.
  • Modern ginning machinery with cleaning and pressing units

Spinning

  • High-volume yarn production
  • Converting ginned cotton into various yarn counts for weaving and knitting applications. We produce ring-spun and rotor-spun yarns meeting international quality standards.
  • Ring spinning frames, rotor spinning machines, winding units

Weaving

  • 35,000+ meters per day
  • Our largest production line manufactures a wide range of woven fabrics including grey fabrics, dyed fabrics, and specialty textiles for apparel, home textiles, and industrial applications.
  • Rapier looms, air-jet looms, warping and sizing machines

Recycling & Waste

  • Full waste-to-product conversion
  • Environmental sustainability through textile waste recycling. We convert production waste into usable materials, reducing environmental impact while creating additional revenue streams
  • Waste processing and recycling machinery
