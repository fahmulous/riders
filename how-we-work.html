How We Work

1

At RIDERS Addis Textile Industry, we operate as a fully integrated textile
manufacturer driven by quality, efficiency, and innovation. From cotton ginning
to yarn spinning and fabric weaving, our processes are streamlined and
interconnected to ensure consistency, speed, and high output.

2

We believe in doing things the right way — through ethical sourcing,
responsible production, and continuous investment in our people and
technology. Our operations are supported by advanced machinery, strict
quality control systems, and a skilled workforce committed to delivering
products that meet both local and global standards.

3

how we deliver — with integrity, innovation, and a long-term vision for growth
