Our Impact in Action

Sustainable Sourcing & Vertical Integration

Our work begins at the source, partnering directly with Ethiopian cotton farmers to ensure a high-quality, traceable supply chain. This vertical integration allows us to maintain strict quality control from the raw material to the final product.

  • Direct support for local farmers
  • Ethically sourced Ethiopian cotton
  • Foundation of our end-to-end value chain

Circular Economy & Waste Reduction

We are committed to environmental sustainability through our modern cotton recycling unit. This initiative allows us to transform textile waste into new, high-quality yarn, significantly reducing our environmental footprint.

  • Modern cotton recycling technology
  • Reduction of textile waste
  • Contribution to a circular economy

Innovation in Textile Processing

Our continuous expansion into wet processing (bleaching, dyeing, and printing) and garment manufacturing is designed to meet the evolving needs of both domestic and international markets. We are building a fully integrated apparel value chain.

  • Expansion into wet processing and dyeing
  • Full-scale garment production
  • Integrated end-to-end apparel solutions

Our Project Process

At RIDERS Textile, we follow a structured approach to ensure every project meets our high standards of quality and client satisfaction. Here's how we bring your textile projects to life:

Step 1: Initial Consultation

We begin by understanding your specific requirements, timeline, and budget constraints through detailed discussions with our textile experts.

Step 2: Concept Development

Our design team creates detailed concepts and material recommendations based on your requirements, providing samples when needed.

Step 3: Prototype Creation

We develop prototypes for your approval, allowing you to see and feel the quality of our work before full production begins.

Step 4: Production Planning

Once the prototype is approved, we create a comprehensive production plan including material sourcing, manufacturing schedules, and quality control protocols.

Step 5: Manufacturing

Our skilled craftspeople and modern machinery work together to produce your textiles with precision and care, adhering to the highest quality standards.

Step 6: Quality Assurance

Every product undergoes rigorous quality checks throughout the production process to ensure it meets our exacting standards.

Step 7: Delivery & Support

We deliver your finished products on time and provide ongoing support to ensure your complete satisfaction.
