OrganizationalStructure

Clear governance and management structure across corporate and operational units

Three-Tier Leadership Model

Riders Trading and Manufacturing S.C. operates with a clear three-tier governance and management structure: Board of Directors providing strategic oversight, Corporate Management coordinating cross-sector operations, and Sector Management Teams executing day-to-day operations in Textile Industry, Farming & Agriculture, Real Estate & Construction, and Financial Services.

This structure ensures accountability, enables specialization, and maintains alignment between shareholder interests and operational execution.
