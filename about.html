About Us

Founded in 2021, Riders Trading & Manufacturing S.C. is one of Ethiopia’s fastest-growing industrial groups. In 2022, we acquired the former ELSE ADDIS Textile Manufacturing Company from the Development Bank of Ethiopia for a total cost of 1.29 billion Ethiopian Birr. This strategic move enabled us to build one of the country’s few fully integrated textile operations — from cotton ginning to yarn spinning, fabric weaving, and recycling.

The Adama facility was originally established in 2010. Today, our 80,000 sqm facility operates as a professionally managed, world-class textile company. With ETB 1 billion registered capital and 1,160+ shareholders, Riders stands on a strong foundation of local investment. Our facility employs 900+ skilled professionals, producing over 20 tons of yarn and 35,000+ meters of fabric daily. What sets Riders apart is our ability to merge international quality standards with deep local roots, building long-term partnerships with Ethiopian cotton farmers while delivering consistent, sustainable, and globally competitive products. Our core services include ginning, spinning, weaving, and cotton transportation.
