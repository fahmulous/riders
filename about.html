Who We Are

Riders Trading and Manufacturing S.C. is Ethiopia's pioneering shareholder-driven diversified holding company, established in 2021 with a bold vision: to create sustainable wealth through strategic investments across high-growth sectors while contributing meaningfully to national economic transformation."

"With ETB 1 Billion in registered capital and a community of 1,160+ shareholders, we represent a new model of collective investment—combining the democratic strength of broad ownership with the strategic discipline of professional management. Our structure allows individual investors to participate in large-scale industrial and agricultural ventures that would be impossible to access alone.

Our Organizational Structure

Built on Solid Principles

FOUNDATION

Riders Trading and Manufacturing S.C. was incorporated in 2021 as a share company under Ethiopian commercial law. Our founders recognized that Ethiopia's rapid economic growth demanded new investment vehicles that could mobilize domestic capital at scale while maintaining accountability to shareholders.

The company's structure follows the successful model of Ethiopian diversified holding companies, with a clear separation between governance (Board of Directors), strategic oversight (Corporate Management), and operational execution (Sector Management Teams).

Shareholder-Driven Governance

OWNERSHIP STRUCTURE

With 1,160+ shareholders, Riders represents one of Ethiopia's broadest investment communities. Our shareholders range from individual investors to institutional participants, united by shared confidence in Ethiopia's economic potential.

Regular General Assembly meetings ensure shareholder voices guide major strategic decisions, while our elected Board of Directors provides continuous oversight. This governance model balances democratic participation with the agility needed for competitive markets.

Our flagship textile operation represents vertical integration at its finest. Located in Adama Industrial Park, Riders-ELSE operates:

  • Cotton Ginning: 20 tons per day processing capacity, supporting our own farms and local farmers
  • Spinning: High-quality yarn production for domestic and export markets
  • Weaving: 35,000+ meters daily production capacity serving Ethiopian and international buyers
  • Recycling: Waste-to-product operations supporting sustainability goals

This sector serves as the cornerstone of our diversified portfolio, generating consistent revenue while supporting Ethiopia's textile export ambitions.

A life estate is a type of ownership that grants an individual (known as the life tenant) the right to possess and use a property for the duration of their lifetime. After the life tenant's death, the property typically reverts to a remainderman or reversioner as specified in the deed or will. Condominium ownership allows individuals to own a specific unit within a multi-unit building or development while sharing ownership of common areas and amenities with other unit owners. Condominium owners typically pay homeowners' association (HOA) fees for maintenance and management of common areas.
