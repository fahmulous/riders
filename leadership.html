Our Leadership Team

Riders Trading and Manufacturing S.C. is led by experienced professionals who combine deep industry expertise with commitment to shareholder value. Our three-tier leadership structure—Board of Directors, Corporate Management, and Sector Management—ensures strategic oversight, operational excellence, and accountability across all business units.

Our Leadership Philosophy

    
Excellence in Execution

We believe that exceptional results come from an unwavering commitment to quality and precision. Our leaders set high standards and empower our teams to meet them through continuous improvement and attention to detail.

    
Sustainable Innovation

We balance traditional Ethiopian textile craftsmanship with modern manufacturing techniques. Our leadership team actively pursues innovations that enhance efficiency while preserving our cultural heritage and environmental responsibility.


Collaborative Growth

We foster an inclusive environment where diverse perspectives drive better outcomes. Our leaders prioritize transparent communication, mutual respect, and shared success across all levels of the organization.

"Our leadership philosophy isn't just about managing a textile company—it's about creating lasting value for our customers, employees, and communities while honoring Ethiopia's rich textile tradition."
